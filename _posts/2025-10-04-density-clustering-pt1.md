# Ridesharing Is Caring — Geospatial Density Clustering (Part 1)

October 4th, 2025

![NYC retro taxis](..\assets\images\posts\2025-10-04-density-clustering-pt1\Dodge_Polara_and_other_Yellow_Cabs_in_1973_NYC.jpg)

This is Part 1 of two blog posts that I'm writing to highlight my recent projects using density-based clustering to explore geospatial datasets and urban dynamics. In this first post, I will define and analyze taxi ridesharing efficiency using a public dataset of NYC yellow cab rides, and in the second post, I will extend this approach to analyze urban density patterns across towns and cities in the US and internationally.
